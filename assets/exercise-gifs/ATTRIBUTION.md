# Exercise GIFs

These 67 animations adapt the three-frame illustrations from
[Workout Guide](https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide) at commit
`aac599224bb9780305239607ef98540b7e0ce389`, by Bryl Lim and
[Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data).

Changes: frames 1, 2, 3, and 2 were sequenced at 350 ms each, composited
against the application's purple background, and encoded as looping GIFs.
